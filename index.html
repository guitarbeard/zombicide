How the odds work

The roll
Each die hits on its Accuracy or higher, so one die hits with chance p = (7 − accuracy) / 6. With n dice, the chance of at least k hits is Σ C(n,i) · pⁱ · (1−p)ⁿ⁻ⁱ for i from k to n.
The math follows depesz's The hard math of killing zombies in Zombicide.
Hits and Damage
A hit only kills a zombie the weapon has enough Damage for. Walkers and Runners need 1, Brutes need 2 and Abominations need 3. The odds count hits, so check Damage against what is in the Zone.
Re-rolls
Plenty of Bullets and Plenty of Shells each re-roll the whole attack once, for weapons that use that ammo. Lucky adds one more. The odds assume you re-roll only when you come up short, so the chance becomes 1 − (1 − P)^(re-rolls + 1).
Card counts belong to the Survivor and stay put when you switch weapons.
Survivors
Pick a Survivor to add their skills. Blue skills always count; tick Orange and Red ones as they unlock. +1 die, +1 to dice roll, +1 damage, Lucky, Sniper, Ambidextrous and Roll 6: +1 die Combat all change the numbers. Skills don't apply to car attacks.
Cars and Molotovs
Car odds cover one Zone of a Drive Fast attack, which can also hit a second Zone. A Molotov doesn't roll. It kills everything in the Zone, so it is the only weapon that shows 100%. Any roll can miss, so rolled odds always stop short of it.
The weapons
Every weapon in the 2nd edition core box: starting gear, the Equipment deck, the Epic weapons and both cars, with stats from the cards. Ma's Shotgun and Gunblade have a melee and a ranged side. Hover a trait like Sniper or Reload for its rule, and tap a row in Head to head to set the zombie count.
